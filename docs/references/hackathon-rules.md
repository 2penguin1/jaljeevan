# Environmental Hacks: hackathon rules

Source: https://www.wemakedevs.org/aws/env (fetched 2026-10-10)

- **Event:** Environmental Hacks, WeMakeDevs × AWS, Bharat Builds Tour Event 02. Online, **Oct 8–11, 2026**.
- **Our track:** **Heat and Water**. The track lists heatwaves, floods, monsoon waterlogging, droughts, water tankers, leaks and groundwater. Each track is judged separately.
- **Team:** 3 people (1–4 allowed). University students in India, verified on AWS Builder Center.

## Hard requirements
- **AWS:** to win a prize the project must "either use at least one AWS open source tool or be deployed on AWS". The two paths are *Build It* (AWS open source tools, run locally) and *Ship It* (deployed on AWS).
- **A 3-minute recorded demo video** showing what it does, who it is for, and where AWS fits. **There is no live demo, so the video is all the judges see.**

## Judging criteria (no weights given)
1. **Idea and Impact:** "Does it fix a real environmental problem? And what changes for the people living with it?"
2. **Built on AWS:** required to win.
3. **Design and usability:** could someone outside the team use it?
4. **Execution:** "One feature that runs beats five that almost do."
5. **Demo video.**

Judges score what was built, not what was spent.

## Optional
- **Blog** on AWS Builder Center, linked in the submission. The top 5 blogs win AirPods.

## Prizes
- **Track winner:** ₹2,00,000 plus $2,000 in AWS credits.
- **Top 4 runners-up:** $1,000 in credits each.
- **Top 10 students:** fast-track Amazon interviews.

## AWS credits
- A new account gets up to $200 in free-tier credits.
- The team leader can request $25 more.
